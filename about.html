About Me_

Research Scholar • Differential Geometry

> I am a Research Scholar at Jadavpur University, where I completed both my bachelor's and master's degrees. I began my PhD in August 2024 and am currently pursuing my research in the field of differential geometry.

> My academic interests include contact geometry, spacetime geometry, geometric flows, solitons, and differential geometry more broadly. I have a strong enthusiasm for learning and exploring new ideas, particularly those that deepen our understanding of geometric structures and their applications.

> This website serves as a platform to document and showcase my academic and research journey, including my ongoing work, publications, interests, and future developments. I invite you to explore the Research section to learn more about my current and past work.

> I am always interested in connecting with researchers, students, and others who share an interest in geometry and related areas. If you find any aspect of my work interesting, I would be glad to discuss ideas, exchange perspectives, and explore opportunities for academic collaboration.

> As this website will continue to evolve alongside my academic journey, I will be adding new research, projects, resources, and other academic materials over time. I warmly welcome your comments, suggestions, and constructive feedback, which I hope will help make this platform more useful and engaging. Thank you for visiting, and I hope you find something of interest here.
