About Me

Research Scholar • Differential Geometry

> I am a Research Scholar from Jadavpur University, where I have completed my B.Sc. and M.Sc. degrees in Mathematics. I am currently pursuing research in the field of differential geometry. My primary interests include Riemannian, semi-Riemannian, and contact geometry, as well as geometric flows and solitons. I am driven by a deep enthusiasm for exploring new mathematical concepts and expanding our understanding of complex geometric structures and their applications.

> This website serves as a living archive of my research journey, documenting my ongoing work, publications, and future developments. I invite you to explore the Research section for a closer look at my current and past projects.

> I am always eager to connect with fellow researchers, students, and anyone who shares a passion for geometry. If my work resonates with you, I would be glad to discuss ideas, exchange perspectives, and explore opportunities for collaboration.

> As my work evolves, I will continuously update this platform with new research, projects, and resources. I warmly welcome your comments and constructive feedback to help make this site more engaging and useful. Thank you for visiting—I hope you find something of interest here.
